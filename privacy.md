Autonomous Enterprise uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which articles people read, how they find the site, and how they engage with it. Google Analytics may set cookies and receive information such as the pages visited, referring sources, browser and device information, and interactions with the site. The site’s analytics tag is configured to disable Google signals and ad personalization. For more on Google’s processing and available controls, see [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Autonomous Enterprise Metrics is a private reporting tool for the blog. With the site operator’s Google authorization, it reads aggregate Google Analytics and Search Console reports, including page views, traffic sources, search queries, clicks, and impressions. It writes the results to a private Google spreadsheet for editorial and traffic analysis. The reporting tool does not publish those reports, use them for advertising, or sell them. The spreadsheet is refreshed with recent reporting periods. The site operator can delete the stored reports and [revoke the tool’s Google access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

If you contact us by email, we use your message and email address to respond. For privacy questions, contact [djankov@gmail.com](mailto:djankov@gmail.com).
